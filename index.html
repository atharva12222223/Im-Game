How to Play

1

👥 Gather Your Group

Get 3–15 players together. One phone is all you need!

2

⚙️ Choose Settings

Pick number of players, imposters, and a word category.

3

🃏 Flip Your Card

Each player taps to see their secret word. One (or more) will see "IMPOSTER" instead!

4

🗣️ Discuss & Describe

Take turns describing the word vaguely. The imposter must bluff without knowing the word!

5

🗳️ Vote & Reveal

Vote on who you think the imposter is, then reveal the truth!
